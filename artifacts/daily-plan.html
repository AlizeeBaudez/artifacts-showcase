One mindset shift: Targets set slightly below your peak capacity aren't a downgrade — they're a floor you can hit even on a depleted day. Consistency compounds faster than occasional maximums. Raise each target by 25 kcal once you've closed it 10 days in a row.

On exercise minutes: on session days (Mon, Tue, Wed, Fri) a 25-min workout closes the ring automatically. Thursday's 15-min mobility flow counts too. Saturday and Sunday are intentionally low — a walk or active errand gets you there. The 30 min target is what happens naturally when you do the session, not an extra effort on top.
